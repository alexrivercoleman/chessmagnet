Local patch (chessMagnet), active only when CHESS_SLIDER_TABLES_ON_HEAP is
defined (chess_lib.h defines it for every build):
  The rook/bishop "magic bitboard" attack tables (RookAttacks, 800 KB, and
  BishopAttacks, 41 KB) are allocated with `new` in attacks::initAttacks()
  instead of being static arrays. Static arrays must fit in the ESP32-S3's
  512 KB internal RAM, which they can't; heap allocations above 4 KB go to
  the 8 MB PSRAM automatically. Search chess.hpp for "chessMagnet patch".
